SnapLedger · Web application · Companion to the mobile concept
The same four jobs, at desk width.
Five screens at 1400 × 880. Nothing new is introduced — setup, the review queue, the business-or-personal call and paper capture are the same flows, re-cut for a surface where a list and its detail can sit side by side.
What changes at this width
The queue stops being a card stack and becomes a list beside a detail pane. Two mobile screens — the item and its reasoning — collapse into one view, because there is room to show the working without a second tap.
What does not change
One decision per item, confidence and reasoning on every match, an audit trail, and no in-the-moment work. Desk width buys density, not extra steps — the same three items are still the whole weekly job.
Where the camera goes
A laptop has no useful camera for a till receipt, so paper capture becomes drag-and-drop plus a hand-off to the phone. It is the one screen with no direct mobile equivalent.
